Controls & tips

Keyboard: WASD / arrows drive · Space handbrake · Q winch · E plank · P photo · H horn · R recover · C camera · M map · J journal · G garage · T emotes

Touch: drag the left pad to steer, hold GAS / BRAKE on the right. Drag the view to look around, pinch to zoom.

Gamepad: left stick steer · RT gas · LT brake · A handbrake · X winch · Y plank · B recover.

Find fallen meteorites to power the portal to the next region. Climb to viewpoints to reveal the map, visit cabins to customise your truck, open supply crates for upgrades and photograph wildlife for your compendium. Use planks to bridge streams and the winch to pull yourself (or a friend) out of trouble.
